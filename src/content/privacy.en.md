# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how **Pluma Agência e Produtora Audiovisual LTDA** ("Pluma", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects the personal data of individuals who interact with our contact forms, digital advertisements, and communication channels.

This policy has been drafted in compliance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13.709/2018 — LGPD), and with reference to the standards of the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 2. Data we collect

### 2.1 Data you provide

When you fill out a contact form in our advertisements (LinkedIn Ads, Meta Ads, Google Ads) or reach out to us directly, we may collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- Company name
- Job title or role
- Information about whether you already have a scheduled event

If you choose to contact us via WhatsApp, we collect the phone number you use for that communication and the content of the messages exchanged.

### 2.2 Data collected automatically

When you browse our website `live.pluma.ag`, we automatically collect:

- IP address
- Browser type and operating system
- Pages visited and time spent on them
- Traffic source (for example, whether you came from a LinkedIn ad or a Google search)
- Interactions with buttons and page elements

This collection happens through the following third-party services:

- **Google Tag Manager** (tag management)
- **Google Ads** and **Google Analytics** (campaign measurement and behavior)
- **Meta Pixel** — Facebook and Instagram Ads (campaign measurement)
- **LinkedIn Insight Tag** (campaign measurement)

## 3. How we use your data

We use your data exclusively for the following purposes:

### 3.1 Commercial contact

- Respond to your request for a quote or inquiry
- Send personalized commercial proposals by email
- Maintain communication about the progress of a project

### 3.2 Marketing and remarketing

- Create custom audiences on advertising platforms (Meta Ads, Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads) to display new relevant advertising to you
- Exclude you from campaigns once you have converted into a customer
- Analyze in an aggregated way which campaigns generate the most interest, without individual identification

### 3.3 Service improvement

- Analyze navigation patterns to improve the website and user experience
- Optimize content and layout based on aggregated behavior

## 4. Data sharing

**Pluma does not sell, rent, transfer, or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes.**

Your data is accessed exclusively by Pluma's partners for one-to-one commercial service. No employee, freelancer, or external partner has access to the lead database.

The only exceptions are the technology providers that support our operation:

- **LinkedIn**: when you fill out a form via a LinkedIn ad, the data is collected on the platform and transferred to us
- **Supabase**: database infrastructure where leads are stored with restricted access
- **Google Workspace**: email communication with you
- **WhatsApp** (Meta Platforms): messaging communication when you opt for that channel

These providers act as **data processors** (under LGPD) and have their own privacy policies.

We may also share data when required by court order, competent authority, or legal obligation.

## 5. Storage and retention period

Your data is stored in our internal database (Supabase) with access restricted to Pluma's partners, protected by authentication and encryption both in transit and at rest.

**Retention period:** data is kept for an indefinite period as long as there is a legitimate commercial purpose, or until you request its removal. You may exercise your right to deletion at any time (see item 7).

## 6. International transfer

Some of the providers we use have servers outside Brazil (for example, Google, Meta, and LinkedIn, with infrastructure in the United States and Europe). This transfer occurs with contractual and technical safeguards required by LGPD.

## 7. Your rights as a data subject

Under LGPD (art. 18) and, when applicable, GDPR, you have the following rights:

- **Confirmation and access:** know whether we process your data and obtain a copy of it
- **Correction:** request correction of incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data
- **Anonymization, blocking, or deletion:** request removal of your data
- **Portability:** receive your data in a structured format to transfer to another provider
- **Deletion of data processed based on consent**
- **Information about sharing** with public and private entities
- **Withdrawal of consent** at any time

To exercise any of these rights, send an email to **contato@pluma.ag** with the subject "LGPD/GDPR — Data Subject Request" and the specific request. We will respond within 15 business days.

## 9. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website uses first-party and third-party cookies for:

## 10. Information security

We adopt technical and administrative measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or improper disclosure, including:

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/SSL)
- Database access authentication
- Restricted access through identity controls
- Regular backups

No system is 100% secure, but we follow the best practices available. In the event of a security incident affecting your data, we will notify you and the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) within the legal deadlines.
